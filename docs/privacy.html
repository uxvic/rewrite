Privacy

The short version: by default, nothing leaves your Mac.

What we collect

Nothing on a central server by default. No analytics, no tracking, and no account unless you opt into a provider that needs one.

Where your text goes (depends on the engine you pick)

  • Built-in AI (default): processed entirely on your device by Apple's on-device model — not sent anywhere.
  • Open-source local (Ollama): processed locally on your Mac.
  • Free models (newsletter): you enter your email and verify a code; your email is added to the maintainer's newsletter, and the text you rewrite is sent to the maintainer's gateway and an upstream model (e.g. Google Gemini). The gateway stores only a sign-in token and daily usage counts — not your text.
  • Claude (API): sent to Anthropic using your own API key, which is stored in the macOS Keychain.

Dictation

Voice input is transcribed with Apple's Speech framework, used only while you actively dictate.

Permissions

Microphone/Speech are used only during dictation. Accessibility is used only to copy your selection and paste the rewrite back when you use the in-place hotkey.

Your choices

Choose Built-in AI or Ollama to keep everything on-device. For the newsletter option, unsubscribe anytime via the email link, and "Sign out" in the app to delete the local token.
