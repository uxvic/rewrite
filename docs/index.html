Install in 30 seconds

1.
Download Rewrite.dmg, open it, and drag Rewrite to Applications.
2.
First launch: macOS may say the app "cannot be verified" (it isn't notarized yet). Right-click → Open → Open, or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
3.
Click the ✦ icon in your menu bar (or press ⌥Space) and start rewriting.
